PaQueueKa

A queue app for badminton and pickleball open play, on iOS and Android.

I designed it, built it and ship it myself. It takes the whiteboard, the clipboard and the “who’s next?” arguments out of open play.

App Store Google Play paqueueka.info

Why I built it

I play badminton. Open play is the best part of the week and the most chaotic: twenty people, three courts, and a host trying to remember who has waited longest.

Every group solves it with a whiteboard or a paddle stack, and every group argues about it. I wanted a phone to do the fair part, so the host gets to play too. PaQueueKa started as a fix for my own sessions. It now runs badminton and pickleball, with fees, clubs and rankings on top.

Under the hood

One codebase for both stores, a Postgres backend, and a lot of tests, because a queue that’s wrong once loses the room.

App
React Native with Expo and Expo Router
Language
TypeScript
Backend
Supabase: Postgres, auth and edge functions
Live updates
Realtime Postgres changes, so every phone sees the same courts
State
Zustand
Tests
About 5,600 Jest tests in about 600 suites
Beyond sessions
Discover for finding clubs nearby; clubs, persistent rankings and a shared ledger with Pro Host

Sino next? The app knows.

Free to download, no ads, and no account needed to play. Hosts who run a regular group can subscribe to Pro Host for clubs, rankings, templates, a saved roster and a shared ledger.
